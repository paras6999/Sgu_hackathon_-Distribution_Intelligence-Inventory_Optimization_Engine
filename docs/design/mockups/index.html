Vitaran: four layout options

Same content in each. Headline numbers are from the demo run (290 lines, 16,071 cases, 40 lots at risk on 29 Sep 2025). Some shipment rows are examples, not run output. Pick one, or mix parts of two. Automation and human checks are shown in every option.

  • 1. Cycle record (the one you liked)
    Salesforce-style record page. One plan, a step bar, tabs for shipments, distributors, stock at risk, what-if and results. Most familiar to a planner.
  • 1a. Path, highlights and guide
    Option 1 with a Lightning path bar, a highlights strip, a next-step guide and an activity feed, plus a utility bar at the bottom.
  • 1b. Console split view
    Option 1 as a Salesforce Service Console: shipment list, detail, and a schematic network diagram for the selected product.
  • 1c. Approval chain
    Option 1 with a sign-off chain (planner, supply lead, finance, dispatch), pre-approval system checks, and proposed automation rules that decide what needs a human.
  • 1d. Scenario comparison
    Option 1 with what-if scenarios side by side: current plan, diesel up 10%, Gurugram offline, demand up 20% in the north. Real optimiser results.
  • 1e. Record with assistant panel
    Option 1 with an assistant beside the record. Answers come from the plan's data: why a shipment exists, what a change would do.
  • 1f. Orders dashboard
    Option 1e, built around the 16 distributor orders in one plan: status, lines, cases, value, credit used, and what changed since the draft. Real values from the live plan (version 2).
  • 2. Guided run
    A wizard. One decision per screen, with the automation choice made once at the end. Easiest to explain to a first-time user.
  • 3. Exceptions queue
    Inbox style. The system does the routine work and shows only what needs a person. Best for showing automation with a human in the loop.
  • 4. Stage board
    Kanban by stage. Each column is a step with its own automation switch. Shows the whole week at a glance.
